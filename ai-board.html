AI Warnings — the Warning–Development Board

Statements by AI companies and public officials about catastrophic or loss-of-control risk, each put through the public-warrant test of Intelligence Without Wisdom: is the event defined, is there a horizon and a probability, is evidence named, is it said what would count against it, and is a stop threshold named?

A low score does not say a warning is false or insincere; it says the statement, as made, does not carry the evidential warrant its form implies. The same test applies to reassurances.
