What AI companies and officials say about catastrophic or loss-of-control risk, each put through six plain questions from Intelligence Without Wisdom: Is the event defined? Is there a deadline? A likelihood? Is evidence named? Is it said what would count against it? Is a stop threshold named?

Why: a warning shapes policy and markets whether or not it can be checked. How to read: a low score does not say a warning is false or insincere - only that, as made, it cannot be tested. The same six questions apply to reassurances.
