What is automatic and what is decided by a person

Collecting, classifying, the institutional scoreboard, seals and this website are automatic and follow the published rules. Marking an individual's statement as “came true” or “did not” is never automatic: the program looks up the official figure and proposes a verdict with its arithmetic, and an editor approves it. Confirming a U-turn or a goalpost move is always an editor's decision.
