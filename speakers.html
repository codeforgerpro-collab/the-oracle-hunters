Report Cards

One card for every person and institution on the record: how much of what they say about the future can be checked, how ambiguous it is, and how it turned out.

An institution card (a ministry, a press office) collects official statements that name no individual speaker. A calibration score is shown only after 10 checked forecasts. Labels judge the statement as made, never the person.

All times are UTC. Page updated 13 Nov 2026 21:12 UTC. Every quote is a short excerpt that links to its official source. Methodology: Professor Dimitrios Thomakos (Ars Prognostica; Intelligence Without Wisdom) · A Tekmerion project · Sister site: ATHENA News Room.
