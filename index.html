How it works: when a deadline passes, the program reads the statement with fixed, published rules, finds the official figure (World Bank, Eurostat, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Bank for International Settlements) and settles it - automatically, with its arithmetic shown on the statement's page. Statements about subjects with no official yearly figure (security, AI, climate, most company plans) stay on the record but cannot be settled this way; they are counted in the last column. The rules · every entry is sealed daily and anchored in Bitcoin - latest seal in block #976,357.

How to read this Each box is one sentence a public figure or institution said about the future, word for word, with a link to the official text. Can be checked means it names what will happen, by when, and how likely - so it can be compared with official figures later. Too vague to check means it does not. Ambiguity (0-100) is how many different ways the sentence can be read. When the date passes, the forecast is compared with official data and marked came true, partly or did not come true. Labels judge the sentence, never the person. Full guide and glossary.
