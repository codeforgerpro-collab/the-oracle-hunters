About

Evidence, not opinion.

The Oracle Hunters (Οἱ Κυνηγοὶ τῶν Χρησμῶν) keeps what governments, central bankers, presidents and AI leaders say about the future on the public record - and checks it when it falls due. It is a Tekmerion product, built by Antonios Vlachos on a forecasting-judgement framework published at the Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CAIL) of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens - see the methodology.

The name and the seal

The name

In ancient Greece a chresmos (χρησμός) was the answer an oracle gave about the future. People with power consulted the oracle before they went to war, founded a city or changed a law, and they acted on what it said. Its answers carried great authority, and they were often open to more than one reading. Herodotus tells of King Croesus, who was told that if he attacked Persia he would destroy a great empire. He did, and the empire he destroyed was his own.

Today the voices with that authority are governments, central banks, presidents, international bodies and the people who lead technology and AI. What they say about the future moves markets, budgets and lives. The Oracle Hunters, in Greek Οἱ Κυνηγοὶ τῶν Χρησμῶν, are the people who go after those statements. We do not mock, guess or predict. We find each statement in its official source, record it word for word, note how clearly it can be checked, and when its date arrives we compare it with the official figures.

The seal

The seal shows the tripod of Delphi, the bronze seat on which the Pythia, the priestess of Apollo, gave the oracle's answers. Across its legs flies an arrow, the weapon of Apollo the far-shooter and of every hunter. Together they say what this site does: the prophecy is put to the test.

The rim reads "The Oracle Hunters · Every prophecy on the record", our promise in a single line. It takes the form of a seal on purpose. In antiquity a seal proved that a document was genuine and had not been changed. Here every day of the record is sealed with a cryptographic fingerprint and anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain, so anyone can prove that no entry was altered after the fact.

The colours are those of the site. Gold stands for a record that lasts, night blue for the unknown future, and moon silver for the light by which a hunter finds the way.

What The Oracle Hunters is - and is not

It is

  • A record of statements about the future, kept until they fall due
  • Forensic: every label shows the rule that produced it
  • The same tests for every party, country, optimist and pessimist
  • Checked against official figures, with the arithmetic shown

It is not

  • A judgement of anyone's intentions, honesty or character
  • Activism, commentary or a fact-check of past claims
  • Investment advice
  • Paid for by anyone it scores

Principles

Evidence

Every conclusion points to its source and to the rule that produced it.

The method is written down, checkable and the same for every country; the data is open.

Human judgement

The tools support the analyst; they never replace them. Any automatic verdict can be corrected.
