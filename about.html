About

The Oracle Hunters (Οἱ Κυνηγοὶ τῶν Χρησμῶν) audits the people whose words move markets and policy: fact-checkers judge whether past claims are true; we keep what influential people say about the future on the record and check it when it falls due.

It is forensic, not activist. We do not judge intentions, honesty or character — only whether a statement, as made, can be tested, how ambiguous it is, and how it turned out. The site is built automatically from official sources and every decision on resolution is made by a human editor.

Legal notice

We process statements that public figures and institutions made in their public role, for journalistic, academic and research purposes (GDPR Art. 85; Greek Law 4624/2019, Art. 28). We publish short excerpts with a link to the full official text. Nothing here is investment advice. To ask for a correction or to reply, use the corrections page.
