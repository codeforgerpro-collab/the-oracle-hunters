Who stands behind The Oracle Hunters

About

The Oracle Hunters (Οἱ Κυνηγοὶ τῶν Χρησμῶν) is a Tekmerion product. It is built by Antonios Vlachos on the forecasting-judgement framework of Professor Dimitrios Thomakos, whose research is published at the Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CAIL) of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens.

Co-founder · product and engineeringAntonios Vlachos

Designs and builds The Oracle Hunters and the other Tekmerion products: the collection of official sources, the classification rules in code, the checks against official data and this website. Long professional experience in financial-markets trading and audit, with a focus on risk and on judging information under uncertainty.

Responsible for product, engineering, daily operation, editorial approval of every verdict about a person.
Co-founder · methodologyProfessor Dimitrios Thomakos

Department of Business Administration, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens; Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. Author of Ars Prognostica and of the working paper Intelligence Without Wisdom. Designs and checks the measurement methods, the indicators and the validation criteria used across Tekmerion products.

Responsible for the framework: kinds of claim, the three axes, testability, ambiguity and the six questions for AI-risk claims.
The company Tekmerion

A Greek start-up in formation. It builds analysis tools that turn large volumes of official and public data into evidence-based, checkable and understandable assessments, for educational, academic and professional use. The name is the ancient Greek tekmerion: the proof that supports a conclusion.

Responsible for publishing The Oracle Hunters, its corrections policy and its right of reply.

The Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory of the Department of Business Administration, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. Intelligence Without Wisdom (November 2026) is a CAIL working paper, and Ars Prognostica carries the laboratory's affiliation.

What The Oracle Hunters is - and is not

Fact-checkers judge whether past claims are true; we keep what influential people say about the future on the record and check it when it falls due. It is forensic, not activist. We do not judge intentions, honesty or character - only whether a statement, as made, can be tested, how ambiguous it is, and how it turned out. The same tests apply to every party, every country, optimists and pessimists alike.

Principles

  • Evidence — every conclusion points to its source and to the rule that produced it.
  • Transparency — the method is written down, checkable and the same for every country.
  • Human judgement — the tools support the analyst; they never replace them.
