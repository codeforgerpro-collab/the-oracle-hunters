About

Evidence, not opinion.

The Oracle Hunters (Οἱ Κυνηγοὶ τῶν Χρησμῶν) keeps what governments, central bankers, presidents and AI leaders say about the future on the public record - and checks it when it falls due. It is a Tekmerion product, built by Antonios Vlachos on the forecasting-judgement framework of Professor Dimitrios Thomakos, published at the Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CAIL) of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens.

What The Oracle Hunters is - and is not

It is

  • A record of statements about the future, kept until they fall due
  • Forensic: every label shows the rule that produced it
  • The same tests for every party, country, optimist and pessimist
  • Checked against official figures, with the arithmetic shown

It is not

  • A judgement of anyone's intentions, honesty or character
  • Activism, commentary or a fact-check of past claims
  • Investment advice
  • Paid for by anyone it scores

Principles

Evidence

Every conclusion points to its source and to the rule that produced it.

Transparency

The method is written down, checkable and the same for every country; the data is open.

Human judgement

The tools support the analyst; they never replace them. Any automatic verdict can be corrected.
