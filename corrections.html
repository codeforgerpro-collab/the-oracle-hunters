Corrections & right of reply

Anyone quoted here - and anyone else - can ask for a correction or reply to an entry. This is our policy.

What you can ask for

  • A quote that is wrong, cut so that it changes the meaning, or credited to the wrong person.
  • A wrong date, place or source link.
  • A label or verdict you believe is wrong under our published rules.
  • A reply of your own to an entry about you, published on this page.

What we do

  1. We check the request against the official source and the published rules.
  2. If we were wrong, we correct the entry and log the correction here and in the change history, with the date and what changed.
  3. If the entry is right under the rules, we explain why, with the rule and the figure.
  4. A reply from a person quoted is published in their own words, shortened only for length or for content we may not lawfully publish.

What we do not do

  • Remove an accurate quote because it is inconvenient.
  • Change a verdict outside the published rules, or for payment or pressure.
  • Rewrite history: the daily seals keep the original record, so every correction is visible as a correction.

To ask, open a correction request or write to codeforgerpro@gmail.com and include the link to the entry. Every source behind a checked statement is also kept as an Internet Archive copy, linked from the statement's page.
